Our story

We met on a rainy Tuesday in a bookshop, both reaching for the last copy of the same novel. He let her have it. She gave it back a week later with a note in the margin, and neither of us has stopped writing to the other since.

Seven years, two apartments and one very opinionated dog later, we are ready to make it official. We would be honoured to have you beside us.
